# Trademark / brand conflict note — Wingman App

## Source
https://www.wingmanapp.com — Wingman Group, Inc.

## Findings (informational, not legal advice)
- Product concept overlaps: friends/family create profiles and introduce singles.
- Live USPTO registration **WINGMAN** (e.g. Reg. 4,915,114 / Ser. 86542063) covering online dating and social introduction services (Class 45).
- Marketing language can feel thematically similar if Winged sounds like “friends land better dates.”

## Decisions (2026-09 — keep brand)
| Keep | Avoid |
|------|--------|
| Product name **Winged** | “Wingman”, “Wingmom”, “wing man” |
| Role **Wing / Wings** | Framing as generic “friends write your profile and swipe” |
| Wing visual theme, motion, marks | Close taglines like “better dates landed by…” |
| Tagline **Friends plan it. You show up.** | Affiliation / endorsement implications |

## Differentiation emphasis (copy + product)
Lead with mechanics competitors don’t share:
- Live **Deal Room** (3‑minute venue/time lock)
- **Pro Wings** network + **Date Pass escrow** + **Proof of Stay**
- Dual Keep/Skip flash; Bachelor vs Wing shells
- Anti-fraud / Fair-Play signals on Pro hire

## Legal footer
Subtle non-affiliation on welcome + Profile/Me:
> Winged is not affiliated with Wingman App or Wingman Group, Inc.

## Recommended next steps
1. Counsel review of **Winged** + **Wings** role naming vs Reg. WINGMAN.
2. Keep scrubbing “wingman*” strings (scripts, comments, press).
3. Do not copy Wingman App UI, photos, or press assets.
